About Us

About Heong Arena

Heong Arena is an online gaming platform designed to bring gaming tournaments, events, digital gaming products, useful gaming tools and player-focused services together in one place.

Our goal is to provide gamers with a simple and convenient platform where they can discover tournaments, participate in gaming activities, access digital gaming products and stay updated through announcements and notifications.

Our platform currently provides features including gaming tournaments, a digital gaming store, gaming events, useful tools, user accounts and player services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a reliable, accessible and user-friendly gaming platform for players.

We aim to make tournament participation, gaming-related purchases and access to useful gaming services straightforward while maintaining transparency and responsible platform management.

User Experience

Heong Arena allows registered users to maintain a gaming profile containing information such as their username, WhatsApp number, avatar, IGN, Game ID and Zone/Server ID.

Users can also view their tournament history, store history and payment records through their profile.

Payments & Verification

Where payment verification is required, users may be asked to submit transaction information such as their UPI ID and UTR/transaction ID.

Payment records may be maintained for tournament registrations and store purchases to help verify transactions and provide customer support.
