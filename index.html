About Heong Arena

Heong Arena is a mobile-first esports and tournament platform built to provide gamers with a simple and organized way to discover tournaments, participate in events, manage registrations, and keep track of their gaming activities.

Our goal is to create a reliable platform where players can discover competitive gaming opportunities and tournament organizers can manage tournaments and registrations efficiently.

What We Offer

  • Esports tournaments
  • Tournament registration
  • Online payment processing
  • Gaming events
  • Tournament announcements
  • User profiles
  • Game details
  • Registration history
  • Payment history
  • Notifications
  • Account management

Tournament information, event information, and announcements are managed through our platform and displayed dynamically to users.

Our Approach

We focus on building a platform that is simple, mobile-friendly, transparent, secure, easy to use, and deeply focused on the gaming community.

We use modern technologies and security practices to help protect user accounts, payments, and platform data.

Payments

Where paid tournament registrations are offered, payments are processed through supported payment providers such as Razorpay.

Payment verification is handled through secure backend processing to help ensure that registrations are created only after successful payment verification.

Our Community

We want Heong Arena to be a positive and competitive environment for gamers. Players are expected to follow tournament rules, respect other participants, and use the platform responsibly.
